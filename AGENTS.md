# Working rules

`NormalityContract` is the automatic sealed system baseline; `Expectation` is an explicit user market question. Their immutable basis/snapshot must never be recomputed. Deterministic resolvers are authoritative. Every domain, timeline, history, dashboard, brief, and replay market read uses an injected LIVE or REPLAY clock and requires `availableAt <= clock.asOf`. `observedAt`, `ingestedAt`, and `availableAt` are distinct; historical EOD bars may be backfilled later but become admissible only at legitimate publication time. Trading deadlines use `TradingCalendar`; evidence without trustworthy adjusted prices is unsafe and resolves as `UNRESOLVABLE`/`UNSCOREABLE`. Events and audits are append-oriented. Agents may prepare normal/GTT drafts from explicit user terms but may never choose, confirm, execute, or cancel trades. Replay cannot create trading actions. Normal and smart orders execute only through their dedicated owner-scoped confirmation endpoint with immutable payload, expiry, quote, safety-limit, and idempotency checks. PAPER results must always remain labelled as simulated. Frontends display server calculations and must not recreate financial logic. Use observational language only—never unsupported causal claims.
